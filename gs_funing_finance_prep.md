# 🗂️ 6-Week Pre-Joining Plan

---

## Week 1 — Fund Finance Products

### 📘 Learn
- Subscription / Capital Call Facilities  
- NAV Facilities  
- Hybrid Facilities  
- Repo / Secured Financing  
- Warehouse Financing  

### 📚 Where to Learn
- Cadwalader — *Fund Finance 101*  
- Mayer Brown — *NAV Credit Facility Primer*  
- Dechert — *Sub-line vs NAV Facilities*  
- ILPA — NAV facility guidance  

### 🛠️ Output
Create a **2-page cheat sheet**:

| Product | Collateral | Borrower | Key Risk | Protection | Pricing Driver |
|--------|-----------|----------|----------|------------|----------------|

---

## Week 2 — Structured Credit Mechanics

### 📘 Learn
- Tranches  
- Subordination  
- Waterfalls  
- Excess Spread  
- Triggers  
- Overcollateralisation  

### 📚 Where to Learn
- Angel Oak — *Securitization 101*  
- Schroders — *Securitisation Primer*  
- Risk Control — *Lectures on Securitisation*  

### 🛠️ Output
Draw and explain:

> “Where do losses go first, and when does the senior get hit?”

---

## Week 3 — Collateral & Private Market Assets

### 📘 Learn
- Private credit loans  
- PE fund interests  
- Capital commitments  
- Consumer / SME loans  
- Specialty assets  

### 📚 Where to Learn
- AIMA private credit reports  
- Fitch / S&P structured finance criteria  

### 🛠️ Output
Build a **collateral risk map**:

| Asset | Cashflow Source | Default Driver | Recovery Driver | Liquidity | Modelling Issue |
|------|----------------|---------------|----------------|----------|----------------|

---

## Week 4 — Pricing, Haircuts & Risk Drivers

### 📘 Learn
- Advance rates  
- Haircuts  
- Margining  
- Borrowing base  
- Funding vs asset spread  
- Liquidity premium  

### 📚 Where to Learn
- Fitch ABS cashflow model docs  
- Repo / financing primers  

### 🛠️ Output
Create a **pricing template**:

Inputs:
- Collateral value  
- Advance rate  
- Expected loss  
- Funding spread  

Outputs:
- Expected return  
- Stress loss  
- Break-even spread  
- Max safe advance rate  

---

## Week 5 — Build a Simple Cashflow Model

### 📘 Learn
- Defaults  
- Recoveries  
- Prepayments  
- Interest  
- Waterfall allocation  

### 📚 Where to Learn
- Securitisation primers (Week 2)  
- Fitch modelling frameworks  

### 🛠️ Output
Python notebook:
simple_structured_credit_cashflow_model.ipynb

Must include:
- Base case  
- Default stress  
- Recovery stress  
- Prepayment stress  
- Simple tranche waterfall  

---

## Week 6 — Front-Office Deal Thinking

### 📘 Learn
Think in:

> Collateral → Cashflows → Structure → Risk → Pricing → Decision  

### 🛠️ Practice
Do **10 deal cases**:
- Capital call facility  
- NAV loan  
- Repo vs ABS  
- Warehouse financing  
- Specialty finance pool  

### 🛠️ Output
Write **5 one-page investment memos**:

- Transaction  
- Collateral  
- Economics  
- Top 3 risks  
- Modelling approach  
- Recommendation

# ⏱️ Daily 2-Hour Routine

## 45 min — Product / Market Reading
Focus only on:
- Structure  
- Risk  
- Failure modes  

---

## 45 min — Build / Model
- Python / Excel  
- Cashflows / stress / structure  

---

## 20 min — Deal Drill
Answer aloud:

> “Would I finance this? What are the risks? What would I change?”

---

## 10 min — Reflection
Write:
- What I learned  
- What I don’t understand  
- One question to explore
