# 12-Month Skill System for Buy-Side Readiness (2 Hours/Day)

## Purpose

This is not a reading plan.
This is a **skill-building operating system** designed to move from:

**Risk Quant → Investment-Adjacent Quant → Buy-Side Ready Candidate**

Primary development goals:

1. Investment Judgment
2. Structured Credit/ABF Fluency
3. Decision Speed Under Ambiguity
4. Practical Investor Tool Building
5. Market Positioning for Transition

---

# Core Daily Skill Loop (2 Hours)

## Daily Skill Stack

### Skill 1 — Investment Judgment (45 min)

**Objective:** Train capital allocation thinking.

Daily exercise:
Review one transaction / presale / financing case and answer:

1. What is the asset?
2. What drives return?
3. What are the biggest downside risks?
4. What would I diligence?
5. Would I invest / finance it?

### Weekly output target

* 5 investment decisions per week
* 50–60 mini deal cases per quarter

### Skill being built

* Underwriting intuition
* Risk-return judgment
* Deal structuring instincts
* Investment communication

---

## Skill 2 — Structured Credit Fluency (30 min)

**Objective:** Build ABF/structured credit intuition.

### 8-Week Core Rotation

Week 1 — Waterfalls
Week 2 — Credit enhancement / subordination
Week 3 — Defaults and recoveries
Week 4 — Prepayments
Week 5 — Warehouse financing
Week 6 — Repo and haircuts
Week 7 — Capital call facilities
Week 8 — NAV lending

Repeat deeper.

### Weekly output target

* 1 concept mastered per week
* 52 concepts per year

Skill focus:
Understand economically, not academically.

---

## Skill 3 — Decision Speed Under Ambiguity (30 min)

**Objective:** Train front-office cognition.

Daily 2-minute response drill.

Use framework:

* State assumptions
* Identify drivers
* Form provisional view
* State risks/caveats

Example prompts:

* Would you finance this pool at 70% advance rate?
* Rates rise 200bp — what breaks first?
* Why can strong collateral still be a bad trade?

### Weekly target

* 5 ambiguity drills
* 1 drill set refined each week

Skill built:

* Speed of structuring thought
* Commercial judgment under uncertainty
* Interview and investment reflexes

---

## Skill 4 — Reflection / Learning Journal (15 min)

Daily write:

* What I learned
* What I missed
* Where reasoning was weak
* What I improve tomorrow

Compounding mechanism.

# Career Training Agent Layer

This skill system should operate like a personal career training agent with four modes.

---

## Mode A — Deal Drill

Use when daily practice is needed.

Prompt:
"Give me today's structured credit / fund finance / private credit deal case. Make me decide whether to invest or finance it."

Expected output:

* One realistic case
* Clear but incomplete information
* Need to make assumptions
* Forced recommendation

Skill trained:
Investment judgment under imperfect information.

---

## Mode B — Ambiguity Drill

Use when decision speed is the focus.

Prompt:
"Give me one front-office ambiguity question. Force me to answer in 2 minutes using assumptions, drivers, provisional view, and risks."

Skill trained:
Fast structuring under pressure.

---

## Mode C — Investment Committee Challenge

Use after writing a memo or deal view.

Prompt:
"Act as an investment committee. Challenge my recommendation, assumptions, downside case, structure, liquidity risk, and commercial judgment."

Skill trained:
Defending views under scrutiny.

---

## Mode D — Career Steering

Use weekly or monthly.

Prompt:
"Based on my recent work, assess my progress toward becoming an investment-adjacent structured credit quant. Identify the weakest skill and adjust next week's training plan."

Skill trained:
Adaptive career compounding.

# 100-Deal Case Progression Curriculum

This is the apprenticeship track.

The objective is not to read 100 deals.
The objective is to make 100 decisions.

Each case must end with:

* Invest / finance / pass
* Key reason
* Top three risks
* One diligence request
* One stress scenario
