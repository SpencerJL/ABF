# ArxNova Interview

## Since we last met, what made you stay interested in the role, and how do you now see yourself fitting into what we’re building?

- Since we last met, I’ve reflected more on why this opportunity stands out to me, and I think there are really three
  reasons.

- First, I’ve been interested for quite a long time in moving closer to credit and asset-backed investing. Even back in
  my time at Howden, I found myself most drawn to portfolio behaviour, credit performance, downside analysis, and how
  quantitative work can inform real investment decisions. So for me, this role is not a random move — it’s actually very
  consistent with where I’ve wanted to develop.

- Second, I think ArxNova is a very rare opportunity because of the stage the platform is at. It’s not just joining an
  established process — it’s a chance to help build the quantitative and analytical foundations of the platform early
  on. That’s very appealing to me, because I enjoy building things from scratch and creating tools that become part of
  how a team actually invests and manages risk.

- Third, I was very positively struck by the people. From meeting you and Luca, I got the impression of a team with deep
  experience, but also a very clear vision for what you want to build. That matters a lot to me. If I were to make this
  kind of move, I would want to do it with people I can learn from and with a platform whose direction I genuinely
  believe in.

- In terms of fit, I don’t see myself as someone who would just provide technical support in the background. What
  excites me is using quantitative tools to improve investment decision-making — whether that’s analysing collateral pools,
  stress-testing assumptions, identifying risk drivers, or building systematic frameworks that help the team evaluate
  opportunities consistently. So I see my role as helping bridge analytics and investment judgment, while also building
  infrastructure that can support the fund as it grows.
